Density is the new acreage

When parking, cooling, and future halls eat the parcel, traditional solar loses the land fight before interconnection even starts. Janta’s towers track the sun and stack harvest vertically—so constrained commercial and industrial sites can still put meaningful clean capacity next to load.

In the field

Munich Airport is piloting three-dimensional energy production with Janta—proof that footprint-sensitive infrastructure can still host serious generation.

Why it matters

Airports, data campuses, and industrial yards share one constraint: space. Vertical density turns that constraint into a design input—not a hard stop.
